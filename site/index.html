Song → Sampler Kit

Any song
becomes a
playable kit.

kitCreator separates a track into its instruments, transcribes every note played, and rebuilds it as a multi-octave sampler instrument — exported straight to SFZ and DecentSampler, ready to load in any sampler or DAW.

Try the real demo → See how it works ↓
Signal Path
01

Separation

Demucs + BS-RoFormer isolate drums, bass, and pitched instruments from the full mix.

02

Transcription

Basic Pitch and CREPE extract every note, onset, and pitch played on each stem.

03

Timbre Match

CLAP embeddings cluster round-robins and fill gaps between recorded notes.

04

Export

ADSR + loop points are estimated, then packaged as SFZ and DecentSampler presets.

One song in. A full instrument out.

Point kitCreator at a track and an instrument — drums, bass, guitar, piano, or synth — and get back a kit that spans the full keyboard, velocity-layered, round-robined, and ready to trigger from any MIDI controller.

FormatsSFZ · DecentSampler
InstrumentsDrums · Bass · Guitar · Piano · Synth
RunsLocal CLI, no upload required
$ kitforge build --song track.wav \
  --instrument "lead synth" --range C2-C7
 
✓ separation  htdemucs_ft
✓ transcription  basic_pitch
✓ timbre cluster  clap · hdbscan
✓ export  synth_kit/kit.sfz
